Privacy Policy – Khata Book

Khata Book is a ledger app for shopkeepers. This is a template: add the date and your contact email before publishing.

Your data stays on your device

Customer details (name, phone, address, area), entries and receipt photos you add are stored only on your own device. The app has no account, no server and no analytics, and the developer cannot see your data.

What the app can access

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to attach a receipt photo.
  • Phone / WhatsApp / SMS: the app only opens those apps with a pre-filled message when you tap the buttons; you send it yourself.
  • Files: only when you export or import a backup.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → "Delete all data from this device", or uninstall the app.
